# itos 0.5.0

## Upgrading

1. **Drop the ledger checks that test itos rather than your project.** A `done_when` check belongs
   in your ledger when it would fail because your code or your config changed. One that would
   fail only if itos changed tests itos, and itos is proven before each release by its own
   conformance corpus and features, run against the tarball you install. Remove:
   - checks that feed itos a synthetic config or ledger (through `ITOS_CONFIG`, `--config` or
     `--ledger` on a scratch file) and expect its verdict, messages piped into
     `itos commit check-message -` or `itos hook commit-msg` under one included;
   - checks that run `itos verify` or `itos ci plan` over a recorded range of commits, and above
     all any `itos verify <sha> HEAD`: its range grows with every commit, and so does its time.
     The commit-msg hook and CI's `verify` already re-check every commit you make;
   - checks of the shape of a `--json` output.

   Keep the checks of your own config: `itos config check`; a message or a path your `itos.yaml`
   must reject, checked against it by `itos commit check-message -` or `itos commit check-paths`
   (your commit policy, your scopes); and `itos version --check`, where the release you pin is the
   proof.
   If itos stops doing what its notes say, report it as a bug at
   <https://github.com/donvargax/itos/issues> instead of guarding against it in your ledger.
